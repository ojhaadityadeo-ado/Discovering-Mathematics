Follow the Choices

Each factor (a + b) branches into an a-choice and a b-choice. Paths with the same number of b’s produce the same term; counting them gives its coefficient.

a = left branchb = right branchHighlighted = selected term

C(n, k) = n! / (k!(n − k)!) counts the ways to select k positions out of n. The theorem here uses nonnegative integer powers, with 0! = 1.
