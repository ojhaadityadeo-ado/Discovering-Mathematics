PERPENDICULAR EDGES

An ordinary box.

When all three edges are mutually perpendicular, the base area is |b||c| and the height is |a|.

V = |a||b||c|
COPLANAR EDGES

No height. No volume.

With a in the base plane, its projection onto the normal is zero. The solid flattens into a plane.

a · (b × c) = 0
PARALLEL BASE EDGES

No base. No volume.

If b and c are parallel, b × c = 0. A unit normal from this cross product is undefined, but the volume formula still gives zero.

V = |a · 0| = 0

Keep these three ideas together

Cross for the base.|b × c| measures the parallelogram area. Its direction is perpendicular to the base.

Dot for the height.h = |a · n| takes only the component perpendicular to the base. A slanted edge is usually longer.

Absolute value for the volume.The triple product records orientation with its sign. Volume is always nonnegative.
